FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does a website take?

A landing page takes about a week. A full business website typically takes 2–3 weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on how fast feedback comes in.

What do you need from me to start?

Just your logo, any content you have, and 30 minutes for a kickoff call. Don't have copy or images? We structure and polish everything for you.

Can you redesign my current website?

Absolutely. Redesigns are one of our favorite projects — we keep what works, modernize what doesn't, and make sure nothing about your brand gets lost.

Do you write the copy?

We structure, edit, and polish your message so it reads clean and converts. For full copywriting from scratch, we can add it as an extra.

What happens after launch?

You get a handover with everything you need. Every package includes free tweaks after launch, and maintenance plans are available if you want ongoing support.
